# Pixel Quest Chronicles

A pixel-art Dungeons & Dragons story, one episode every weekday. Four strangers, one road of lanterns, level 1 to level 20. **Every roll you see on screen is real**: the dice are seeded and logged, and you can check any of them on the [Dice](dice.md) page.

## Latest episode: [C1E1 - Lantern Thirty-Seven](episodes/c01-e001.md)

On 3 Emberfall 1047 AK, four strangers crossed paths in Brindle Cross: Tamsin won Wil Fenn's coppers at cards, Ilsevel found two odd entries for Lantern 37 in the inn's ledger, and Brannoc met Oriel at the shrine as Father Tobin gave her his holy symbol. Then Lantern 37 went dark and goblins raided the square. The four fought them off together; Ilsevel fell and was brought back by Oriel, and one goblin was taken alive.

## The party

- **[Brannoc Ironvow](characters/brannoc.md)** - The Shield. Level 1 dwarf fighter.
- **[Ilsevel Starwick](characters/ilsevel.md)** - The Archivist. Level 1 elf wizard.
- **[Tamsin Underbough](characters/tamsin.md)** - The Card Player. Level 1 halfling rogue.
- **[Oriel Ashdown](characters/oriel.md)** - The Acolyte. Level 1 human cleric.
